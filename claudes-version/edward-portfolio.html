A little about me

I'm a full stack engineer and founding builder based in Austin, Texas. I take products from rough whiteboard idea to live in production — backend, frontend, AI, security, and the infrastructure underneath.

I started as the first engineer on a small team and grew into leading a six-engineer org building real-time financial analytics for thousands of daily users. Along the way I shipped LLM-powered editorial pipelines, hardened auth systems, healthcare workflow tools, and a stack of internal products nobody ever sees but plenty of people rely on.

My approach is simple: if it can be built, I'll figure out how to ship it. I'm optimistic by default about new tech, allergic to long specs, and I treat "I haven't done that before" as a starting point, not an excuse.

I'm AI-native by habit — I build with modern tooling because the gap between idea and shipped product has never been smaller, and I'd rather close it than admire it.
